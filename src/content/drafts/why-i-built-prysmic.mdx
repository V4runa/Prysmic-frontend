This file is a draft outline for Eddy. It is not a public post. It is not routed, it is not in the journal index, and it is not in the feed.

Nothing below is a fact about your life. The sentences are a shape. Replace them with what you are willing to stand behind, or delete them.

## What this post is for

A short note, in the first person, about why Prysmic exists. Publish it only after you have written it yourself.

## A shape you could follow

1. The feeling you wanted a tool to have. Calm. A place between the mind and the world, rather than a dashboard.
2. What you kept meeting elsewhere that did not fit. Separate apps. Search instead of tags. Habits as checkboxes. Stay general unless you want to name something.
3. The decision that tags, which a person creates, are how they return. Say why text search is absent, in your own words.
4. Why the calendar is the center. One timeline for notes, moods, contracts, and tasks.
5. Who you hope will feel at home. People who write, journal, and keep a reflective practice.
6. What you do not want Prysmic mistaken for. A productivity system. A self-optimization score. A second brain.
7. An invitation into early access, with no promise about price.

## Lines to rewrite or throw away

These are not your voice yet. They are placeholders so the outline has a cadence.

- I wanted a room, not a dashboard.
- I wanted to come back by the marks I had chosen to leave.
- A habit needed an intention before it needed a streak.

## Leave blank until you write them

- The year or season you started.
- Any practice you actually keep.
- Anyone you want to thank.
- The contact you want at the end.

Do not invent a biography to fill those blanks.
